## 1. The funnel

The mistake most bot devs make is treating YouTube as the goal. It isn't. YouTube is
the **top** of the funnel. The repo is the bottom.

```
   TikTok / Shorts / WA Status   ← daily, 30 seconds, pure "wow"
              ↓  (curiosity)
        YouTube long tutorial    ← weekly, 6-12 min, "how do I get this?"
              ↓  (intent)
      Session site + Fork button ← the only two links that matter
              ↓  (action)
        ⭐ Star  🍴 Fork  📺 Sub
              ↓  (retention)
      Telegram + WhatsApp group  ← where they come back and bring friends
```

**Key insight:** a Short's job is *not* to teach. Its job is to make someone think
*"wait, WhatsApp can do that?"* — and the only way to find out is the long video,
which is the only place the deploy steps live.

## 3. The 5 content pillars

Rotate these. Never post two of the same pillar back to back.

### 🔥 Pillar 1 — "Wait, WhatsApp can do that?" (40% of posts)
The single highest-performing format. One jaw-dropping command, 30 seconds, no talking
needed — just screen recording + text overlay + trending audio.

Best commands for this: `shazam`, `ss`, `imagine`, `vv`, `carbon`, `readqr`, `tempmail`,
`autodl`, `vcf`, `hidetag`, `hangman`, `blackjack`.

### 🛠️ Pillar 2 — "Deploy it yourself" (20%)
The conversion engine. Full walkthroughs: Render, Railway, Koyeb, Heroku, Termux, VPS.
These are the videos that produce forks. Longer, lower views, highest value.

### 😂 Pillar 3 — "Group chaos" (20%)
Relatable group-admin content — `antilink` catching a spammer, `hidetag` waking a dead
group, `warn` → `kick` chains, `tagall`. Highly shareable inside WhatsApp itself, which
is exactly where the target audience already lives.

### 🧠 Pillar 4 — "Free AI on WhatsApp" (10%)
8 providers, no cost. Big hook for the "I can't afford ChatGPT Plus" crowd.
Commands: `ai`, `gemini`, `gpt`, `coder`, `summarize`, `grammar`, `imagine`, `aisearch`.

### 🏗️ Pillar 5 — "Build with me" (10%)
Dev-audience content: writing a custom plugin, the plugin loader, the 633-command
architecture. Low views, but this pillar is what earns **stars from developers** —
and developer stars are the ones that stick.

## 5. Non-negotiable rules

1. **Never post a watermarked TikTok to YouTube.** The algorithm suppresses it. Export
   clean from your editor once, upload the same file everywhere.
2. **First 2 seconds decide everything.** Start on the *result*, then rewind. Never open
   with "Hey guys, welcome back to the channel."
3. **Pin a comment on every video:** the repo link + session site link.
4. **Reply to every comment in the first hour.** Early engagement is the strongest
   ranking signal you control for free.
5. **Every video ends with the same CTA.** Star, fork, subscribe. Same words each time.
6. **Never show your real session ID on camera.** Blur it, always — someone will pause.
7. **One command per Short.** Two commands = zero retention.

- **Reddit** — r/selfhosted, r/nodejs, r/SideProject. Post the *architecture*, not an ad.
- **Dev.to / Hashnode** — one article: "I built a WhatsApp bot with 633 commands and no
  API keys." Cross-link to the repo.
- **Nairaland / Nigerian tech groups** — high-intent local audience.
- **WhatsApp bot Telegram groups** — where the fork-hungry crowd already gathers.
- **YouTube comments on competitor bots** — genuinely helpful replies, never spam.

## 7. What to do when a video pops

If a Short crosses ~10k views:

1. Immediately post a follow-up using the **same command, different angle** within 24h.
   The algorithm has found your audience — feed it the same thing again.
2. Pin a comment: *"Full setup tutorial → [long video link]"*.
3. Make the long-form version that same week.
4. Do **not** change your format because one video worked. Double down, don't pivot.
